For adults 18+. BMI is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis. Maintenance calories use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation plus your selected activity factor; actual needs vary. Recipe calories and macros are planning estimates and vary by brand, cooked yield and preparation. The app will not generate a target below its 1,200 kcal/day female or 1,500 kcal/day male planning floor, but that does not establish medical safety for an individual. Halal and kosher filters screen recipe ingredients only and are not certification. Consult a qualified clinician or dietitian for individual advice.
